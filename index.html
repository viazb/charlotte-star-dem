Since 1982, Charlotte Star Service has been the trusted home for BMW and Mercedes-Benz owners across the Charlotte area. What started as a commitment to honest, expert European auto repair has grown into four decades of loyal relationships built on fair pricing and genuine care.

Dan Failey leads the team with decades of hands-on expertise in German luxury vehicles. At the front desk, Joe is the welcoming face customers rave about — the kind of service rep who actually listens and explains everything clearly, whether you're a car person or not.
