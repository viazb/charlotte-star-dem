Founded in Charlotte

Over Four Decades
of German Expertise

Charlotte Star Service opened its doors in 1982 with a straightforward mission: give BMW and Mercedes-Benz owners in Charlotte a shop they could truly trust. A place where the diagnostic was honest, the pricing was fair, and the work was done right the first time.

More than four decades later, that mission has never changed. We have spent over 44 years becoming deeply fluent in the engineering language of German luxury vehicles — their systems, their quirks, their service histories, and their common failure points. That depth of knowledge is what separates a specialist from a general repair shop.

Customers who have spent years paying dealership rates often tell us the same thing the first time they come to us: "I wish I had known about you sooner." We charge significantly less than the dealership, and because we focus entirely on BMW and Mercedes-Benz, we often know these vehicles better than a large dealership with dozens of technicians spread across every make and model.

We are located at 4225 Monroe Rd in Charlotte — the same address we have called home for decades, serving generations of Charlotte families and their vehicles. When you walk through our door, you are met by people who genuinely care about getting you back on the road safely, at a price that respects your budget.

Every repair, every service, and every diagnostic we perform is backed by our 3-year warranty on all parts and labor. That is our commitment to the quality of our work and our confidence in the people doing it.

Our Focus

Why We Only Do Two Brands

BMW
BMW
All models, traditional combustion engine
MB
Mercedes-Benz
All models, traditional combustion engine
We do not service hybrid or electric vehicles. Our expertise is built entirely around traditional combustion-engine BMW and Mercedes-Benz models.

Most repair shops try to work on everything. We made a different choice in 1982 and have never second-guessed it: we would do only BMW and Mercedes-Benz, and we would do them better than anyone else in Charlotte.

That focus means our technicians spend their entire careers learning the engineering, the electronics, and the service history of exactly two brands. When your BMW's check engine light comes on, we have seen that code hundreds of times. We know the likely cause, the correct fix, and what to watch for.

Specialization also means we stock the right parts, use the right tools, and have the right software for your vehicle — without having to adapt or compromise.

If you drive a hybrid or electric vehicle, we respect that — but we are not the right shop for you. We stay in our lane so we can be exceptional in it.
